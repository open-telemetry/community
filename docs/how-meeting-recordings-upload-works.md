# How meeting recordings work

OpenTelemetry meetings are hosted on Zoom accounts managed through the
[LFX Project Control Center](https://lfx.linuxfoundation.org/tools/pcc/),
see [how to create and configure meetings](how-to-handle-public-calendar.md).

## Where to find recordings

Find recordings and transcripts on the
[OpenTelemetry meetings page](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/opentelemetry),
in the list of past meetings.

## Legacy recordings

Recordings of meetings held before the migration to LFX are not on the meetings page.

Meetings held on the [legacy Zoom accounts](../assets.md#zoom-accounts) were recorded to Zoom
cloud. Their links are on a
[publicly viewable Google spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SYKfjYhZdm2Wh2Cl6KVQalKg_m4NhTPZqq-8SzEVO6s).

This spreadsheet is deprecated: it will become unavailable by the end of 2026, and the recordings
it links to may become unavailable after that date.

Entries older than 6 months were moved out of the public spreadsheet into the private
['Zoom recordings archive' spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1U34Ae4D8EMhMbrFujdSRjoHBae3o9pvptLO1c6X4RPI/edit?usp=sharing),
and the recordings it links to may also be unavailable by the end of 2026.
